CurrentGuard AI

Monitor Power. Prevent Waste. Protect Energy.

CurrentGuard AI is a smart monitoring platform that combines low-voltage IoT sensing, simulated smart meter streams, uncertainty-aware AI anomaly detection, and human-in-the-loop triage. The system does not jump to accusations of power theft; instead, it provides structured evidence for authorised technicians to inspect root causes.

Core Capabilities:

  • Zero-Risk Simulation First: Full workflow testable safely without 230V mains danger.
  • Two-Layer Intelligence: Room-level waste detection + supply-vs-consumer balance comparison.
  • Action-Oriented Triage: Contextual suggestions for facility managers to cut idle consumption.
  • Security by Design: Device tokens and SHA-256 tamper-evident cryptographic audit log.
